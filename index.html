BADMINTON

Badminton is a racquet sport played either in singles or doubles, with the objective of scoring points by hitting a shuttlecock over the net into the opponent's side of the court. The game involves a lightweight racquet and a shuttlecock, played on a rectangular court divided by a net. Matches are typically best of three games, each played to 21 points, and players take turns serving. The sport is governed internationally by the Badminton World Federation (BWF), and it has been an Olympic sport since 1992. Badminton is known for its fast-paced nature, requiring agility, precision, and strategic thinking, making it a popular recreational and competitive activity enjoyed globally for its health benefits and dynamic gameplay.

International badminton

International badminton is a highly competitive and widely popular sport played at the global level. Governed by the Badminton World Federation (BWF), it involves players from various countries competing in singles and doubles formats. The sport demands agility, precision, and strategic thinking as players aim to score points by hitting a shuttlecock over a net. Major international badminton events include the Thomas Cup and Uber Cup for team compe titions, as well as prestigious individual tournaments like the All England Open, BWF World Championships, and the Olympic Games. These events showcase the top talents in the sport and attract a massive global audience. International badminton has evolved over the years, witnessing advancements in equipment technology, game strategies, and an increasing level of competitiveness among nations. The sport continues to captivate fans worldwide with its fast-paced rallies, skillful shots, and the intense athleticism displayed by the players. It serves as a platform for cultural exchange and promotes camaraderie among nations through the shared love for this exhilarating racquet sport..
