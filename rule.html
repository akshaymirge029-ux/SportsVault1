Badminton Rules and Regulations

National Badminton Rules

In national badminton games, the rules are generally based on the regulations set by the national badminton association. Common national rules include:

  • 1. Matches are typically best of three games.
  • 2.Games are played to 21 points, and players take turns serving.
  • 3.Service must be underhand, and the shuttlecock must be hit below the server's waist.
  • 4.Players must adhere to the code of conduct and fair play.
  • 5.Players should be ready for the next rally promptly, and unnecessary delays are discouraged.
  • 6.During serves and service returns, players must ensure both feet are in a stationary position and do not lift off the ground until the shuttlecock is struck.

International Badminton Rules

International badminton games follow the regulations set by the Badminton World Federation (BWF). Key international rules include:

  • 1.The BWF governs the sport internationally and sets standards for equipment, court dimensions, and gameplay.
  • 2.Matches are played in a best-of-three format, with each game played to 21 points (except for the deciding game, which is played to 15 points).
  • 3.The rally scoring system is used, meaning a point is scored on every serve, regardless of which side serves.
  • 4.Players must follow strict rules for service, foot positioning, and other technical aspects.
  • 5.Use of electronic devices, except for officiating purposes, is usually prohibited during matches.
  • 6.Players may request breaks for medical reasons, and the tournament referee has the authority to grant or deny such requests.
