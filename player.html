Position: Mixed Doubles

Country: India

Lin Dan

Position: Singles

Country: China

Achievements:

  • Lin Dan, often referred to as "Super Dan," is one of the greatest badminton players of all time
  • He has won two Olympic gold medals in men's singles, in 2008 (Beijing) and 2012 (London)

Lee Chong Wei

Position: Doubles

Country: Malaysia

Achievements:

  • Lee Chong Wei is a former Malaysian badminton player who held the world No. 1 ranking for a record 349 weeks
  • He is a three-time Olympic silver medalist (2008, 2012, 2016) in men's singles. Lee Chong Wei has won numerous titles, including Commonwealth Games gold and multiple All England Championships.

Carolina Marín

Position: Mixed Doubles

Country:Spain

Achievements:

  • Carolina Marín is a Spanish badminton player who made history by winning the gold medal at the 2016 Rio Olympics in women's singles
  • She is known for her aggressive style of play and has clinched several World Championship titles
